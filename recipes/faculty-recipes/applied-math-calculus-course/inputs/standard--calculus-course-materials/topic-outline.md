# Math 1b — Detailed Topic Outline

## Unit 1: Integration Techniques

### 1.1 Review and Integration by Parts
- Fundamental Theorem of Calculus (brief review)
- Substitution rule (review)
- Integration by parts: ∫u dv = uv − ∫v du
- Choosing u and dv (LIATE heuristic)
- Repeated integration by parts; reduction formulas

### 1.2 Trigonometric Integrals
- Integrals of sin^m(x) cos^n(x)
- Integrals of tan^m(x) sec^n(x)
- Wallis's formula and related results

### 1.3 Trigonometric Substitution
- Substitutions for √(a²−x²), √(a²+x²), √(x²−a²)
- Completing the square before substituting

### 1.4 Partial Fractions
- Proper vs. improper rational functions
- Distinct linear, repeated linear, and irreducible quadratic factors
- Strategy for decomposition

### 1.5 Improper Integrals
- Type I: infinite limits of integration
- Type II: discontinuous integrands
- Comparison test for improper integrals
- p-integrals: ∫₁^∞ x^(−p) dx and ∫₀^1 x^(−p) dx

## Unit 2: Applications of Integration

### 2.1 Area and Volume
- Area between curves (vertical and horizontal slicing)
- Volume by cross-sections (disk/washer method)
- Volume by cylindrical shells

### 2.2 Arc Length and Surface Area
- Arc length formula: L = ∫√(1 + (dy/dx)²) dx
- Surface area of revolution

### 2.3 Physical Applications
- Work: constant and variable force; spring problems (Hooke's Law)
- Hydrostatic pressure and force
- Center of mass and centroids

### 2.4 Probability Applications
- Probability density functions
- Mean, median, expected value via integration
- Exponential and normal distributions (qualitative)

## Unit 3: Sequences and Series

### 3.1 Sequences
- Definition and notation; terms vs. values
- Limit of a sequence; bounded and monotone sequences
- Monotone Convergence Theorem

### 3.2 Introduction to Series
- Partial sums; convergence and divergence
- Geometric series: sum = a/(1−r) for |r| < 1
- Telescoping series
- Divergence (n-th term) test

### 3.3 Convergence Tests
- Integral test and p-series
- Direct comparison test
- Limit comparison test
- Ratio test
- Root test
- Summary: choosing the right test

### 3.4 Alternating Series
- Alternating Series Test (Leibniz's theorem)
- Alternating Series Estimation Theorem
- Absolute vs. conditional convergence
- Rearrangement theorem (statement)

## Unit 4: Power Series and Taylor Series

### 4.1 Power Series
- Definition; radius of convergence; interval of convergence
- Finding radius of convergence via ratio test
- Differentiation and integration of power series (term-by-term)

### 4.2 Taylor and Maclaurin Series
- Taylor series centered at a; Maclaurin series
- Standard Maclaurin series: eˣ, sin x, cos x, ln(1+x), 1/(1−x), (1+x)^k
- Manipulating known series (substitution, differentiation, integration, multiplication)

### 4.3 Taylor's Theorem and Error Estimation
- Taylor's theorem with Lagrange remainder
- Bounding the remainder |R_n(x)| ≤ M|x−a|^(n+1)/(n+1)!
- Approximation problems: how many terms are needed?

### 4.4 Applications of Series
- Computing limits using series expansion
- Evaluating integrals that lack elementary antiderivatives (e.g., ∫e^(−x²) dx)
- Euler's formula: e^(ix) = cos x + i sin x (brief introduction)

## Unit 5: Differential Equations

### 5.1 Introduction to ODEs
- Definition; order; general vs. particular solutions
- Direction fields and qualitative analysis
- Euler's method (numerical approximation)

### 5.2 Separable Equations
- Method of separation of variables
- Exponential growth and decay: dy/dt = ky
- Logistic growth: dy/dt = ky(1 − y/M)
- Newton's Law of Cooling

### 5.3 Linear First-Order Equations
- Standard form: y′ + P(x)y = Q(x)
- Integrating factor method
- Mixing problems; compartment models

### 5.4 Second-Order Linear Equations
- Homogeneous equations with constant coefficients
- Characteristic equation; three cases (distinct real, repeated, complex roots)
- Non-homogeneous equations: method of undetermined coefficients
- Spring-mass system; harmonic oscillator; resonance (qualitative)
