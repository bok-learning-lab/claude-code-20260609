b) [4] If a shot of potassium chloride was delivered such that the extracellular concentrations of both potassium and
chloride increased by 40mM what would happen to the membrane potential? Quantitatively show your reasoning.
Would this make it (circle one) easier / harder / no change for the cell to reach the threshold potential for an action
potential?
The concentrations outside would both equally increase by 40mM for both ions. While the net charge on the outside is
unchanged the Nernst potentials would be different and this would lead to a new, higher, resting potential.
The new Vmem would be: -33.9 mV (show your work)
This voltage level is potentially high enough to be above the threshold for initiating an action potential.
If it’s above the threshold then it would likely interfere with the ability of the voltage sensitive channels to reset.
Therefore either a) further action potentials couldn’t be initiated, or b) the cell would be repeatedly firing action potentials
as fast as possible.
