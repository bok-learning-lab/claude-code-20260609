## Slide 2
Describe the purpose of all four chambers of the heart, the valves within the heart, the pulmonary vein, and the aorta; how do defects in these affect flow and circulation?
How do electrical signals move through the heart and why is the order important?
Draw an ECG and label the waves. Indicate the electrical and mechanical events that produce these waves. Differentiate atrial vs. ventricular abnormalities based on an ECG. How do the waves of the ECG change with heart rate and breathing rate?
What is the primary pacemaker of the heart (SA node), and where is it located?
What is a refractory period and why is it essential to cardiac contraction?
Map the flow of blood throughout the systemic and pulmonary vascular systems starting at the left ventricle. How does pressure vary along this path?
Re-create a
Wigger’s
diagram showing the phases of contraction and relaxation in the left atrium and left ventricle. Correlate the mechanical pressure and volume cycles with the electrical (ECG) cycle of the heart. You should be able to recall approximate pressures, volumes and the timing. You should be able to read the EDV and ESV, EDP and ESP of the left ventricle.
Recreate a pressure-volume loop from a
Wigger’s
diagram and estimate the work done by the left ventricle.
What is pre-load and what is after-load? How do changes in them change the stroke work done by the heart, and how do they change the pressure-volume loop of the work done by the heart?
What might cause increased resistance (afterload)? What might cause increased pre-load (filling)?
What is the Frank-Starling Mechanism, and how does it relate to cardiac output and/or stroke volume?
Calculate mean arterial pressure (MAP), ejection fraction (EF), stroke volume (SV), stroke work (SW), cardiac output (CO), heart rate (HR in units of beats per min (BPM)), pulse pressure (PP), total peripheral resistance (TPR).
What are a few biomedical interventions that can alleviate heart dysfunction? In other words, what are some biomedical engineering devices that assist cardiac function (e.g. LVAD, pacemaker), and what do they change about cardiac function?
