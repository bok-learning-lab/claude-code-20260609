What is Biomedical Engineering?



 We integrate fundamental engineering disciplines with the physical and life
   sciences while drawing on mathematics and computational methods.

This convergence allows us to understand the operation of living systems and to
      design novel solutions to critical problems in medicine and biology.
Biomedical engineering is consistently ranked one of the
most valuable majors based on pay, growth in salary, and
   wealth of job opportunities, and impact on others.

        • Increases workload of the

          hypertrophy and heart failure
Physical Laws Describing Blood Flow - Resistance


Example – Which vessel has the highest resistance?
Physical Laws Describing Blood Flow - Resistance
• Flow rate is proportional to the pressure drop (∆P) & inversely
  proportional to resistance

   • Flow = ∆P / R  R = ∆P / Flow
   • Resistance is directly proportional to length of vessel (L) and
     viscosity of blood (η)
       • Inversely proportional to 4th power of radius (r4)
           • So diameter of vessel is very important for resistance

• Poiseuille's Law describes factors affecting blood flow:
   • Resistance = ∆P = 8ηL
                     Flow      πr4
   • Vasodilation dramatically decreases resistance, increases
     blood flow
   • Vasoconstriction does opposite

  Tuesdays (11:59pm). They will typically cover the three
