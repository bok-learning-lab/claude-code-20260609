## Slide 30
Course expectations: What to expect for the course
We hope to interact and engage with you and want you to do the same with us and your peers. A small part of the course grade is based on participation, of which attendance is a component.
We will work in small groups during “lecture” time, office hours, and in your lab groups. We hope you get to know many of your peers in this class.

## Slide 31
Course expectations: What to expect for the course
P Sets will be released a week in advance and due on Tuesdays (11:59pm). They will typically cover the three preceding lectures and readings.
Students may consult with one another on problem sets and share general strategies. This is encouraged but each student's problem set must represent his/her/their own work. Copying from one another is not permitted.
Please try to be as inclusive as possible. Not everyone already knows someone in this class. We encourage you to get to know as many others as you can.
You should also strive to honor the diversity of your classmates.
