                                                                                 Increasing afterload
                                                                                 •   Longer isometric phase
                                                                                 •   Higher isotonic force
                                                                                 •   Lower isotonic velocity




Muscle shortening against a fixed weight (i.e. “afterload”)
1) After the start of stimulation force exerted by the muscle begins to increase
• If the force from the muscle is insufficient to lift the weight then the length of the muscle stays constant
• isometric contraction = active force generation with no change in muscle length
2) When the force becomes high enough to lift the weight, the muscle gets shorter
• Initially, the contraction occurs at a constant velocity
• Isotonic contraction = muscle contraction with constant velocity
