Part III: Muscle work, power and Hill’s model

Muscle does measurable mechanical work only when it displaces a load. This mechanical work (W) is the product of load (F) and displacement (Δ x ). Power (P) is the rate at which work is performed, or work per unit time (Δ t ). 

The velocity-tension curve reveals an interesting relationship between muscle power and applied load.

Q: What is the power of the contraction? How do we find it from a force-velocity curve?

Note: Power is maximal at intermediate loads (where both F and v are moderate) and falls to zero at maximum load (where v = 0) and at zero load (where F = 0).
