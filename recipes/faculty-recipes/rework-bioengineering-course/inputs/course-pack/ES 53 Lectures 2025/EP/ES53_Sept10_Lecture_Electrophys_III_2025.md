## Slide 14
Clinical Case: Hypokalemia
Hypokalemia is caused by abnormally low levels of potassium in the body.
Almost 98% of potassium is found inside the cells. Small changes in the level of potassium that is present outside the cells can have severe effects on the heart, nerves, and muscles.
Potassium is important to maintain muscle contraction.
The kidneys are the primary organs that control the balance of potassium by removing excess potassium into the urine.
