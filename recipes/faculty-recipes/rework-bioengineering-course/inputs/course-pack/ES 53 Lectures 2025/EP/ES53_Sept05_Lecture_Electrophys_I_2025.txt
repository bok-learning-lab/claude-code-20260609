(For reference) Permeability,
conductance, voltage, current
• Permeability (P) refers to how easily a substance can
  cross through the membrane. Units are in cm/sec.
• Conductance (g) refers to ions and how easily they flow
  through the membrane. Conductance is also defined as
  the degree to which the membrane conducts electricity (or
  ions). Units are in siemens.
• Resistance (R) is the inverse of conductance. Units are in
  ohms.
• Voltage (V), or voltage potential is the difference in charge
  across some barrier, and in our case the barrier is the cell
  membrane.
• Current (I) is the flow of ions.
• Recall V = I*R or I = V*g
