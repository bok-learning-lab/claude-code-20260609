## Slide 4
Cardiac Cycle – Volume parameters you need to know
End-diastolic volume
is volume of blood in ventricles at end of diastole (EDV)
End-systolic volume
is amount of blood left in ventricles at end of systole (ESV)
Stroke volume
is amount of blood ejected from ventricles during systole (SV = EDV-ESV)
Ejection fraction
is the portion of blood ejected from the LV compared to the total volume of blood at the end of diastole. (EF = SV/EDV)
Cardiac output
is the volume of blood ejected from the LV per minute. (CO = HR x SV)
