Calculation of GFR using Starling equation
It is possible to calculate glomerular filtration rate
(GFR) using the Starling equation:
    GFR = Kf([Pc − Pi] − [πc − πi])
The equation is used both in a general sense for all capillary flow, and in a specific sense for
the glomerulus:
Note that ([Pc − Pi] − [πc − πi]) is the net driving pressure, and therefore the net filtration is
proportional to the net driving pressure.

Renal Clearance
• Refers to ability of kidney to remove substances from
  blood and excrete them in urine
• Occurs by filtration and by secretion
• Secretion is opposite of reabsorption--substances from
  vasa recta are transported into tubule and excreted
• Reabsorption decreases renal clearance; secretion
  increases clearance
Renal Plasma Clearance (RPC)
• Is volume of plasma from which a substance is
  completely removed/min by excretion into urine
• If a substance is filtered but not reabsorbed then all
  filtered will be excreted RPC = GFR
• If substance is filtered and reabsorbed then RPC <
  GFR
• If substance is filtered but also secreted and excreted
  then RPC will be . > GFR (=120 ml/ min)

Measurement of Renal Plasma Flow

• Not all blood delivered to glomerulus is filtered
  into glomerular capsule
  • ~20% is filtered; rest passes into efferent arteriole
    and back into circulation
  • Substances that aren't filtered can still be cleared by
    active transport (secretion) into tubules
Total Renal Plasma Flow Using PAH
Total Renal Plasma Flow Using PAH
• PAH clearance is used to measure total plasma flow
   • Normally averages ~600 ml/min
   • It is totally cleared by a single pass through a nephron
   • So it must be both filtered and secreted
   • Filtration and secretion clear only molecules dissolved in
     plasma
        • To get total renal blood flow, amount of blood occupied by
          erythrocytes must be taken into account
        • ~40% blood is RBCs; 60% is plasma
        • ∴ total renal blood flow = PAH clearance
                                            0.60
            • = 600mL/min/0.60 = 1 L/min
Clearance of Urea

• Urea is freely filtered into glomerular capsule
• Urea clearance calculations .demonstrate how the kidneys
       . a substance: RPC = V x U/P
  handle
    • V = 2ml/min; U = 7.5 mg/ml of urea; P = 0.2 mg/ml of urea
• RPC = (2ml/min)(7.5mg/ml)/(0.2mg/ml) = 75ml/min
    • Urea clearance is 75 ml/min, compared to clearance of inulin
      (120 ml/min)
        • Thus 40-60% of filtered urea is always reabsorbed
• Is passive process because of presence of carriers for facilitative
  diffusion of urea
