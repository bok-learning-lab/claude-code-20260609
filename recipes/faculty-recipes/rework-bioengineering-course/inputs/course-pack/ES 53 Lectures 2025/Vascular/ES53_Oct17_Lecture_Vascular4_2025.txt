  BP are controlled by aperture

    can result in increased BP

Regulation of Blood Flow To Tissue
 • Two words: “Hypoxic vasodilation”
    • Tissue is low in oxygen (hypoxia)  blood vessels
      dilate
    • Vasodilation increases blood flow which reduces
      hypoxia.
       • This is the key peripheral mechanism for
         regulation of blood flow in the circulatory
         system.
    • The opposite (Hypoxic vasoconstriction) occurs in
      the Pulmonary circulation. Why would that be?
       • Think about the purpose of the pulmonary
         circulation versus the systemic circulation…
Baroreceptor Reflex (Stretch reflex)
 • Is activated by changes in BP
   • Which is detected by
     baroreceptors (stretch
     receptors) located in aortic arch
     and carotid sinuses



    Increase in BP causes walls of these regions
     to stretch
    Baroreceptors send APs to cardiac control
     centers in medulla
Is most sensitive to decrease in BP & and sudden
 changes in BP
