2. [6 pts] Compare and contrast skeletal muscle cells with cardiac muscle cells.

a) [2] Why can tetany not be reached in cardiac muscle cells?

Because the action potentials of cardiac cells have long absolute refractory periods before they can stimulate
another action potential to contract again. Since the action potentials of simultaneous contractions are too far
apart, cardiac muscle cells cannot respond fast enough to frequent stimuli to reach tetany.

3. [5 pts] One form of arrhythmia is fibrillation. Below is an example ECG from a patient with atrial fibrillation.

a) [2] Is atrial fibrillation or ventricular fibrillation more dangerous? Why? Explain in one sentence.

Ventricular fibrillation is more dangerous because the ventricles are responsible for pumping the blood to the
systemic organs (including the brain). If the ventricles do not contract in a coordinated way then very little to no
blood will be pumped. If the atria do not contract in a coordinated way the ventricles won’t fill quite as
effectively before systole, but blood will still get pumped to the body if the ventricles are properly contracting.
