# Mini Quiz — Boron Ch 4: Regulation of Gene Expression

_Five questions, ~five minutes. Source: [chapter-04-regulation-of-gene-expression.md](../../../inputs/textbooks/medical-physiology/chapter-04-regulation-of-gene-expression.md) (Boron & Boulpaep, *Medical Physiology* 3e, ch. 4, pp. 73–101)._

## 1. The **TATA box** is part of which element of a typical eukaryotic gene?

A. The 3′ UTR.
B. **The core promoter**, ~25 bp upstream of the transcription start site.
C. An intron near the polyadenylation signal.
D. The locus control region of a globin cluster.

## 2. **Enhancers** differ from core-promoter elements in that they…

A. must lie within 50 bp of the transcription start site.
B. can act over **long distances (kb to Mb)** and from either orientation, typically via DNA looping that brings transcription factors to the promoter.
C. are required for general transcription factor assembly on every gene.
D. function only in prokaryotes.

## 3. **Heavy CpG-island methylation at a gene's promoter** is most often associated with…

A. transcriptional activation of that gene.
B. **transcriptional silencing** of that gene — methyl-CpG-binding proteins recruit HDACs and chromatin remodelers.
C. no change in transcription, only in mRNA stability.
D. premature termination of translation.

## 4. **CREB** (cAMP-response-element binding protein) is a transcription factor in which structural class?

A. Zinc finger.
B. Helix-turn-helix homeodomain.
C. **Basic leucine zipper (bZIP)** — two helices that dimerize via a leucine "zipper" coiled-coil, with basic regions reading DNA in the major groove.
D. Winged-helix forkhead.

## 5. Which step in the textbook's **eight-step gene-expression diagram** is typically regulated by **mRNA stability elements (AREs)** and **microRNAs**?

A. Alteration in chromatin structure.
B. Initiation of transcription.
C. Nucleocytoplasmic transport.
D. **mRNA degradation.**

---

## Answer key (instructor)

1. **B.** TATA box is part of the **core promoter**, ~25 bp upstream of the TSS. TFIID's TBP subunit binds it. (§2, "core element"; Fig 4-6.)
2. **B.** The defining features of enhancers vs. core elements: long-range action, orientation-independence, and DNA looping. (§2, "Enhancers and silencers act over long distances.")
3. **B.** CpG-island methylation at promoters → MBD-protein recruitment of HDACs and chromatin remodelers → silencing. (§3, "DNA methylation silences gene expression.")
4. **C.** CREB is the canonical bZIP example, along with AP-1 (Fos/Jun) and C/EBP. (§3, "Specific TF structural motifs.")
5. **D.** AREs and miRNAs act at the mRNA-stability step (step 8 in Fig 4-4). The trap is C (nucleocytoplasmic transport), which is a different regulatable step. (§1, Table; Fig 4-4.)
