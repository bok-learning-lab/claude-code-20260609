\textbf{Problem 1} (15 pts). Solve the system $Ax = b$ for every value of $k$:
\[
A = \begin{pmatrix}1&2&k\\0&1&3\\2&5&k+6\end{pmatrix}, \quad b = \begin{pmatrix}1\\2\\5\end{pmatrix}.
\]

\bigskip

\textbf{Problem 2} (15 pts). Let $T: \mathbb{R}^3 \to \mathbb{R}^3$ be defined by $T(x) = Ax$ where
\[
A = \begin{pmatrix}2&0&1\\0&3&0\\1&0&2\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Find the eigenvalues of $A$. Is $A$ diagonalizable? If so, diagonalize it.

\bigskip

\textbf{Problem 3} (15 pts). Apply Gram--Schmidt to $\{(1,1,0,0),(1,0,1,0),(0,0,1,1)\}$ to produce an orthonormal basis for the subspace they span in $\mathbb{R}^4$.

\bigskip

\textbf{Problem 4} (15 pts). The Fourier series of a $2\pi$-periodic function $f$ is given by
\[
f(t) \sim \frac{a_0}{2} + \sum_{n=1}^{\infty}(a_n \cos nt + b_n \sin nt).
\]
\begin{enumerate}
    \item[(a)] Write formulas for $a_n$ and $b_n$.
    \item[(b)] Compute the first three nonzero terms of the Fourier series of $f(t) = t$ on $(-\pi, \pi)$.
\end{enumerate}

\bigskip

\textbf{Problem 5} (15 pts). Solve the system of differential equations
\[
\frac{dx}{dt} = \begin{pmatrix}-2&1\\1&-2\end{pmatrix}x, \quad x(0) = \begin{pmatrix}2\\0\end{pmatrix}.
\]

\bigskip

\textbf{Problem 6} (15 pts). State and prove the Rank-Nullity Theorem.

\bigskip

\textbf{Problem 7} (10 pts). Give an example of a $3\times 3$ matrix that is not diagonalizable over $\mathbb{R}$ but is diagonalizable over $\mathbb{C}$. Justify your answer.
