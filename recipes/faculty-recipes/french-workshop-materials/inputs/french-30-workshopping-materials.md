# French 30/40 — Workshopping Materials + Bok Supplement

_Transcribed from the handwritten "Project as Recipe" card ([`french-30-workshopping-materials.jpg`](french-30-workshopping-materials.jpg)) by Claire-Marie Brisson, Summer of Claude faculty workshop, Day 1 (2026-06-08)._

## Ingredients — your inputs

- French 30 — all handouts / presentations / course site outputs have been downloaded.
- Boucar Diouf (*Déconnecter*, 2025) is the main text we are using to demonstrate different ways of speaking in French, including:
  1. Discours indirect classique
  2. Discours indirect libre
  3. Transposing oral → textual French (how to speak vs. type / formal vs. informal register)

## Instructions — prompts, skills, scripts, tools

- **Skills** — AI re-created "Boucar Diouf" (Senegalese-Québécois author) that students can speak and type to.
- **Prompt** — "amorces" (sentence fragments) that help students articulate ideas using grammar / linguistic functions (spoken & textual).
- **Tools** — podcast + textual outputs.

## Serving — your output

Serving the overall architecture of French 30 (and eventually 40) that gets students to use multimodal tools to:

1. Critique AI outputs & compare with authentic materials (video + text).
2. Talk about the discoverability of francophone content and the limitations of searchability.
3. Work with civil discourse and ask challenging questions of the author in low-stakes format.
