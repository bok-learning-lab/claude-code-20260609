Présentation du cours
Objectifs du cours
● analyser et étudier les cultures francophones par
le biais des médias ;
● renforcer les stratégies de communication avec
des projets collaboratifs : épisodes de podcast et
rédactions journalistiques.
● revoir et élargir le vocabulaire français ;
● revoir et pratiquer les points de grammaire
avancés (les pronoms, le subjonctif, les temps du
passé…)
● développer la narration, la description et
l'expression d'opinions.

Présentation du cours
Syllabus
Attendance : you are allowed a maximum of three absences during the semester. Three
tardies to class will equal one absence.
French immersion : This course is taught exclusively in the French language. You are
expected to speak only in French during class.
Assignments :
Podcasts :
Three episodes, one episode in an interview format.
Journalistic writing :
You will create a blog where you will write three entries in a journalistic style.
Examens :
Three written exams to assess your ability to effectively write in French about topics related
to the course material while using appropriate grammatical structures and vocabulary.
Final Panel and Debate :
You and your classmates will perform a live journalistic panel discussion at the end of the
course
+ Homework assignments / Quizzes
Recommended Book
Robert Collins English-French Dictionary, unabridged edition.
Morton, Jacqueline. English Grammar for Students of French. Ann Arbor, MI: Olivia & Hill
Press, 2013.
