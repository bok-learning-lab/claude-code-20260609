# Gradebook Visualization for Study Strategy Identification

_Transcribed from the handwritten "Project as Recipe" card ([`gradebook-visualization.jpg`](gradebook-visualization.jpg)), Summer of Claude faculty workshop, Day 1 (2026-06-08)._

## Ingredients — your inputs

- Anonymized grade information (AY26, AY25).
- Dates / names of assessments and how much they count in the overall grade.
- Syllabus which includes how grade is calculated.

## Instructions — prompts, skills, scripts, tools

> I am the instructor of a college-level math course. I want to show students an animation made from the past 2y of grade data (attached), so they can visualize grade outcomes as the semester progresses, starting from their first assessment (a mini-exam) and ending with a final letter grade. I want this to be clear and non-alarmist, allowing students to see a more realistic picture of where they may end up and what sort of intervention might be effective.

## Serving — your output

An animation illustrating students' grade outcomes.
